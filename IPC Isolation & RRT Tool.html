What each precaution means

Colors match common isolation door signs: blue Airborne, red Droplet, green Contact, grey Standard, purple Special. Neither WHO nor CDC sets official sign colors, so check your facility's signage.

    Reference: Siegel JD, Rhinehart E, Jackson M, Chiarello L, and the Healthcare Infection Control Practices Advisory Committee. 2007 Guideline for Isolation Precautions: Preventing Transmission of Infectious Agents in Healthcare Settings, Appendix A: Type and Duration of Precautions Recommended for Selected Infections and Conditions. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention; last updated September 11, 2026. cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/appendix-a-type-duration.html

    Added from other guidance (not in Appendix A): MERS-CoV, Candida auris, mpox, novel influenza A, Zika, chikungunya (CDC); Rift Valley fever (WHO); Alkhurma hemorrhagic fever (Public Health Agency of Canada). The RRT activation pathway in part 2 comes from your RRT tool.

    Reference aid only. Always follow your facility's infection prevention policy and current CDC guidance; entries marked "Special" or "See linked entry" point to separate guidance.
